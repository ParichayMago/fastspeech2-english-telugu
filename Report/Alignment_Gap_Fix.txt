Alignment-gap correction and feature rebuild - 3 November 2026

Status: complete; rebuilt targets are selected in config/English/preprocess.yaml and config/Telugu/preprocess.yaml.
Feature version: gap-fixed-v2. All extraction, verification and model smoke checks ran on the local Mac CPU. No cloud resource was started or modified.

Cause and repair
tgt omitted empty TextGrid intervals. Duration sums excluded internal gaps; upstream mel clipping to those sums removed speech tails and shifted per-phone targets. Reading empty intervals and filling any timestamp-only gaps now preserves them as sp silence tokens. Leading/trailing silence still trims normally. Rounded relative interval endpoints cover the full cropped timeline, preserve all non-silence phone identities and reject overlaps. No aligner retraining or split reselection was needed.

All selected examples were re-extracted (rather than patching durations alone): mel, WORLD DIO/StoneMask pitch, energy and durations; phone metadata and training-only normalization statistics were regenerated. The prior two English unusable-pitch examples remain rejected. No new data or speaker identity claims were added.

Native torch.stft replaces CUDA-only Fourier convolution so extraction works locally. Centered reflect padding, periodic Hann window, 1024 FFT/window, hop 256, 22.05 kHz, 80 mel bins, 0-8000 Hz and log floor 1e-5 are preserved. Regression magnitude max absolute error versus the original convolution is 1.67e-6 on a deterministic signal.
