English inference pacing and pauses

Updated the local English inference frontend without changing model weights,
training data, feature arrays, speaker labels, vocoder weights or any cloud
resource. Best checkpoint: english-finetune-pilot-20261104, 2,500 additional
updates from parent step 12,000. Speaker en_est_0155, universal HiFi-GAN.

The former custom-text path normalized an entire passage to a single phoneme
sequence, discarding punctuation boundaries. The new custom-text default plans
clauses before normalizing them. Word order is checked against whole-text
normalization, and every phone must survive vocabulary encoding. Word-internal
hyphens and contractions do not introduce pauses. Numeric punctuation and common
abbreviations do not create false boundaries. Unpunctuated clauses are capped
at 30 normalized words per model call.

Each clause is synthesized separately. Predicted continuous phoneme durations
are multiplied by 1.2 BEFORE rounding, with at least one frame per spoken phone.
The decoder is rerun with those adjusted durations; pitch and energy still come
from the model. This is resynthesis, not stretching the finished waveform.
It does not train question intonation or create a punctuation-conditioned model.

Explicit inter-clause waveform silence: comma 0.14 s, colon/semicolon 0.24 s,
period 0.40 s, question/exclamation 0.45 s. No trailing pause. Overflow clauses
use 0.16 s. Five-millisecond boundary fades reduce join clicks. No denoising,
noise gating, spectral filtering or loudness normalization is used. Per-clause
predictions are saved individually; inserted silence samples are separately
recorded and are not misreported as acoustic model mel frames.
