Separate models and Adam optimizers alternate updates; no model weights are shared. Batch size 8, gradient clip 1.0, full precision, upstream transformer architecture and 4,000-step learning-rate warmup. Seed 20261103. Grouped, shuffled training data are sorted within groups; partial final batches retained. Validation uses the fixed held-out splits, with no optimization. Six component/total losses and TensorBoard events are saved. Vocoder synthesis is deferred, so it cannot interrupt or consume the initial training budget.

The session stops at 40 minutes or 10,000 updates per language, whichever occurs first. Checkpoints contain model weights, Adam state, scheduler step, epoch and exact configuration. Resume reshuffles data and is not a bit-exact continuation within an epoch. Checkpoints are saved atomically at step 1 and every 100 updates, plus the final step. Full validation is scheduled every 500 steps and at normal session end. A time-budget-interrupted validation is explicitly marked incomplete. No claim of convergence or acceptable speech quality follows from this initial bounded session.
